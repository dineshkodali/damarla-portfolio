About Me

A practical, people-focused HR professional dedicated to building seamless employee experiences, supporting organisational growth, and aligning talent strategies with operational priorities.

Professional Profile

I am an HR professional with extensive experience supporting recruitment, onboarding, employee engagement, performance tracking, and people operations within a fast-paced education and training environment.

Throughout my work, I have coordinated high-standard hiring activities, engaged directly with candidates and employees at all levels, supported university placement partnerships, and helped leadership teams deliver effective, repeatable people processes. I bring a strong communicative style, a practical approach to problem-solving, and a genuine passion for cultivating positive employee experiences that directly support organisational performance.

How I Deliver HR Impact

In fast-growing organisations, hiring speed cannot come at the expense of candidate quality, and operational efficiency must never compromise the human touch. My approach unites structured recruitment workflows with empathetic employee support:

  • Targeted Talent Acquisition: Partnering with internal hiring managers to translate team needs into clear role scorecards, running targeted campaigns for specialist technical roles (such as AI/ML, programming, medical coding, and Power BI instructors), and ensuring candidates receive prompt, transparent communication.
  • Campus & Graduate Partnerships: Establishing and nurturing institutional relationships with colleges and university placement cells across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana to build sustainable entry-level talent pipelines.
  • Structured Onboarding & Induction: Guiding new starters through organizational processes, expectations, and culture from Day One, eliminating administrative friction, and ensuring 100% documentation compliance.
  • Engagement & Performance Tracking: Monitoring employee engagement and performance information, spearheading recognition initiatives (work-anniversary and new-joiner spotlights), and helping leadership proactively identify retention opportunities.

Academic & Research Grounding

My professional practice is enriched by continuous postgraduate learning at the University of Hertfordshire (2025–2026) and my foundational degree from KL University (2017–2021). I am also the author of the published academic research paper "Impact of Turnover Intention on Work Engagement", which investigates the behavioral relationship between employee withdrawal cognition and workplace engagement.

What You Can Expect From Me: Reliability, active listening, and people-first execution. Whether coordinating complex hiring drives, leading employee engagement initiatives, or maintaining flawless HR documentation, I ensure that people processes run smoothly, ethically, and purposefully.
