Personal Profile & Journey

About Me

A practical, structured thinker who enjoys connecting operational reality with data.

My Working Approach

Throughout my work in HR and operations, I frequently saw teams struggle with the same problem: plenty of data sitting in different spreadsheets, but very little clarity on what it meant for everyday decisions.

When a team experiences high staff turnover or delayed client turnaround, the instinct is often to blame individuals or buy new software. In reality, the issue is almost always broken handoffs, unclear expectations, or unorganised records. I enjoy stepping into those situations, gathering the facts patiently, and creating tools and processes that make people's working days easier.

How I Got Here

I started my professional journey in HR operations and recruitment coordination. That experience gave me a genuine appreciation for the human side of business: how hiring delays impact team morale, why clear induction checklists matter, and how critical it is to protect employee data.

To build deeper analytical rigor, I moved to the UK to complete an MSc in Business Analytics with Consultancy. My postgraduate studies taught me how to structure business problems mathematically, clean messy transactional records, build data models, and communicate recommendations confidently to senior decision makers.
