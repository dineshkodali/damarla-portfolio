# Portfolio Goals

## Professional Career and Personal Branding Goals

### 1. Main Goal

Create a trustworthy professional portfolio that helps UK employers
quickly understand the candidate's background in Business Analytics,
Consultancy, and HR Management.

### 2. Career Goals

The portfolio should support applications for roles such as: - Business
Analyst - Data Analyst - BI Analyst - HR Analyst - People Analytics
Assistant - Business Consultant - Operations Analyst - Process
Improvement Analyst

The final target role should be selected based on the candidate's actual
experience, technical skills, and job preferences.

### 3. Short-Term Goals

-   Build a professional online presence.
-   Organise education and experience clearly.
-   Publish 3--5 credible projects.
-   Create at least one HR analytics case study.
-   Create at least one business analytics dashboard.
-   Create at least one consultancy or process improvement case study.
-   Add a downloadable UK-focused CV.
-   Link the professional LinkedIn profile.
-   Improve confidence in presenting work.

### 4. Skill Development Goals

#### Analytics

-   Improve Excel skills.
-   Learn or strengthen Power BI.
-   Develop SQL fundamentals.
-   Practise data cleaning.
-   Build KPI dashboards.
-   Explain insights in plain English.

#### Consultancy

-   Practise problem definition.
-   Learn requirements gathering.
-   Improve stakeholder communication.
-   Create process maps.
-   Develop structured recommendations.
-   Present assumptions, risks, and limitations.

#### HR

-   Understand HR operations.
-   Improve knowledge of employee lifecycle processes.
-   Learn basic people analytics.
-   Practise workforce reporting.
-   Understand confidentiality and responsible handling of employee
    data.

### 5. Project Goals

Every published project should demonstrate at least one of the
following: - Analytical thinking - Business understanding -
Problem-solving - Communication - Process improvement - HR knowledge -
Decision support

Projects should be realistic and honest. Simulated projects are
acceptable when clearly labelled.

### 6. Professional Branding Goals

The personal brand should communicate: - A strong willingness to learn -
Practical and structured thinking - Interest in business performance -
Understanding of people and organisations - Professional communication -
Reliability and attention to detail

The brand should not present the candidate as a senior expert if they
are applying for entry-level or early-career roles.

### 7. Recruiter Experience Goals

A recruiter should be able to: - Understand the candidate's background
quickly. - Identify suitable skills. - Review practical project
evidence. - Download the CV in one click. - Access LinkedIn. - Contact
the candidate easily. - View the website comfortably on a mobile device.

### 8. Content Quality Goals

-   Use accurate information.
-   Keep language natural.
-   Use UK spelling and formatting.
-   Avoid unsupported claims.
-   Explain technical terms when necessary.
-   Keep project descriptions concise.
-   Update the website regularly.
-   Remove outdated or weak content.

### 9. Future Goals

Possible future improvements: - Add a blog or insights section. - Add
more advanced analytics projects. - Add downloadable project reports. -
Add a simple content management workflow. - Add accessibility and
performance testing. - Add analytics for website visits only if
appropriate and privacy-compliant.

### 10. Definition of Done

The first version is complete when: - All core pages are implemented. -
Content is reviewed by the portfolio owner. - At least three projects
are published or prepared. - CV and LinkedIn links work. - Contact
details are correct. - Mobile and desktop layouts are tested. -
Accessibility basics are checked. - No placeholder text remains. - No
invented achievements, qualifications, or project results are included.
