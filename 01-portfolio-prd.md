# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Professional Portfolio Website

### 1. Product Overview

Build a clean, professional personal portfolio website for a Business
Analytics with Consultancy graduate who has experience in HR Management
and is targeting professional opportunities in the UK.

The website should feel human, credible, simple, and useful. It must not
look like an over-designed AI-generated website or a generic template.

### 2. Primary Purpose

-   Present a clear professional identity.
-   Explain education and HR experience.
-   Demonstrate practical analytics and consultancy capability.
-   Showcase projects and case studies.
-   Support UK job applications.
-   Provide easy access to CV, LinkedIn, and contact details.

### 3. Target Audience

-   UK recruiters
-   Hiring managers
-   Business analysts
-   HR professionals
-   Consultancy managers
-   Potential professional connections

### 4. Suggested Positioning

Primary positioning: Business Analytics, HR Management, and Business
Consultancy.

The positioning must be adaptable for roles such as: - Business
Analyst - Data Analyst / BI Analyst - HR Analyst / People Analytics -
Business Consultant - Operations or Process Improvement roles

Do not claim expertise, certifications, or experience that has not been
verified by the portfolio owner.

### 5. Core Website Pages

#### Home

-   Name and professional headline
-   Short introduction
-   Main career areas
-   Featured projects
-   Clear buttons: View Projects, Download CV, Contact Me

#### About

-   Professional summary
-   Education background
-   Career journey
-   Interests and working approach
-   Career direction

#### Experience

-   HR Management experience
-   Other relevant experience
-   Responsibilities
-   Verified achievements
-   Tools and methods used

#### Skills

Group skills into: - Business Analytics - HR Management - Business
Consultancy - Communication and Stakeholder Management - Technical Tools

Only list skills that can be explained or demonstrated.

#### Projects

Each project must include: - Project title - Business problem -
Objective - Method or approach - Tools used - Findings -
Recommendations - Outcome or expected impact - Project type: academic,
simulated, personal, or professional

#### Education and Certifications

-   Degree
-   University
-   Relevant modules
-   Dissertation or consultancy project
-   Certifications
-   Current learning

#### Insights

Optional section for: - Short professional reflections - Business
analysis notes - HR and people-related insights - Learning summaries -
Industry observations

#### CV and Contact

-   Download CV
-   LinkedIn link
-   Professional email
-   Simple contact form
-   Optional GitHub or project repository

### 6. Functional Requirements

-   Responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
-   Simple navigation.
-   Fast-loading pages.
-   Accessible colour contrast.
-   Keyboard-friendly interactions.
-   Working CV download link.
-   Working external links.
-   Contact form with validation.
-   SEO-friendly page titles and descriptions.
-   Clear error and empty states.
-   No unnecessary animations.
-   No AI chatbot required for the first version.

### 7. Content Requirements

The content should: - Use clear UK English. - Sound natural and
personally written. - Avoid exaggerated claims. - Avoid corporate
buzzwords. - Use short paragraphs and readable bullet points. - Show
evidence wherever possible. - Clearly separate real experience from
academic or simulated work.

### 8. Non-Goals

Do not build: - An AI-generated personal assistant. - Complex dashboards
in the first version. - A social media platform. - A large blog CMS. -
Excessive animation or 3D effects. - Fake testimonials or invented
achievements. - A complicated multi-user admin system.

### 9. Success Criteria

The website is successful when: - A recruiter understands the candidate
within 30--60 seconds. - The professional direction is clear. - Projects
are easy to find and understand. - The CV is easy to download. - The
site works well on mobile. - The content feels authentic and
trustworthy. - The owner can update projects and links without major
redevelopment.
